The process

It runs on what you already have.

No rip-and-replace. No "DOA" hardware bill. We use your existing cameras and stand it up in days.

1

Your existing cameras

Analog or IP — we connect to the feeds already mounted in your stores. Nothing new to buy.

2

On-prem AI appliance

A compact base station processes video locally (Split-AI), so footage stays on-site and bandwidth stays low.

3

Detect & alert

Hazards are detected in real time and routed to the right person via app, email, and text — calibrated zone-by-zone to kill false alarms.

4

Your team acts

The manager on the floor gets a clear, actionable notification — and clears the aisle in minutes, not hours.

🧩

What actually gets installed

A few compact on-prem appliances — about one per 20 cameras — plug into the camera network you already have. They process video on-site, so footage never leaves the building, and they sip bandwidth. No servers to rack, no rewiring, no new cameras — and our field team handles the entire install.
